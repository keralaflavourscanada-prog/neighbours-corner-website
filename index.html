About Us

A Neighbourhood Store With Flavours From Home

Neighbour's Corner is a local grocery store in Peterborough, Ontario, bringing together the flavours and familiar foods of Kerala and South India.

Whether you're looking for everyday pantry staples, authentic spices, snacks, pickles, frozen foods, rice or flours, we're here to help you find your favourites.
