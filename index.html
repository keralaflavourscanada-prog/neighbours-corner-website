Welcome to Neighbour's Corner

Located in Peterborough, Ontario, Neighbour's Corner is a local grocery store bringing the flavours of Kerala and South India closer to home.


From everyday pantry staples and spices to snacks, pickles, frozen foods, rice, flours and more, we offer a selection of authentic products for families and food lovers in the community.
